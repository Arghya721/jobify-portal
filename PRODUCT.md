# Product

## Register

both

## Users

Software engineers across all experience levels — early-career to senior — actively hunting or passively browsing. Primary context: desktop, focused, comparing multiple roles. Secondary: mobile, quick check. They've been burned by LinkedIn spam, fake listings, and recruiter noise. They arrive skeptical and leave loyal if the signal is clean.

## Product Purpose

Direct-from-ATS engineering job board. No recruiters, no sponsored noise, no fake listings. Jobify pulls straight from source so every listing is real, unfiltered, and actionable. Success = engineer finds a relevant role and applies in under two minutes without encountering a single recruiter or ad.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Trustworthy. Direct.

Stripe's calm credibility meets Linear's expert confidence. The interface communicates "we did the work so you don't have to" — clean signal, no performance. Not cold or sterile: warm enough to reduce job-search anxiety, sharp enough that engineers feel respected.

## Anti-references

- **LinkedIn / Indeed** — corporate bloat, ads, recruiter spam, fake "easy apply" that goes nowhere, dark patterns pushing Premium
- **Generic SaaS cream** — beige/white + corporate blue, hero-metric cards, "10,000+ jobs" stat blocks, identical icon-heading-text grids
- **Startup hustle tone** — "We're disrupting hiring!", rocket emojis, aggressive growth-hack copy
- **Glassmorphism / purple-gradient UI** — decorative blur cards, neon accents, anything that looks like a Web3 dashboard

## Design Principles

1. **Trust through transparency** — surface the source, never obscure where a listing comes from. Authenticity is the product.
2. **Signal over noise** — every element on screen must earn its place. If it doesn't help the engineer decide, cut it.
3. **Expert confidence** — design for engineers who know what they want. Dense information presented clearly beats padded layouts that assume ignorance.
4. **Calm authority** — reduce the anxiety of job searching through spatial clarity and unhurried type, not through cheerful illustration or empty reassurance.
5. **Respect time** — fast to scan, fast to filter, fast to apply. Keyboard-navigable, no dead ends.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support throughout — animations enhance, never obstruct. Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements. Sufficient contrast in both light and dark themes.
